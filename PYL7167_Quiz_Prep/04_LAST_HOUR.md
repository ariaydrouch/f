## Five exam traps (the difference between 90 and 100)

1. **Reduced mass.** Any mention of D, μ, π, or \(R_\infty\) vs \(R_H\) is a reduced-mass question.
2. **\(Z^4\)** for fine structure, **\(Z^2\)** for Bohr energies, **\(1/n^3\)** for Rydberg spacings and SO.
3. **\(\Delta J=2\) is E1-forbidden.** Count lines before drawing them.
4. **Geometry of SG.** Write \(s=\frac{\mu}{M}(\partial B/\partial z)(L/v^2)(\ell+L/2)\) and \(2s\). State which length is the magnet.
5. **Spherical ≠ Coulomb.** \(m\)-degeneracy from spherical; \(\ell\)-degeneracy from \(1/r\).

## If a derivation is asked, this is the skeleton

**Rydberg from correspondence.** Classical \(E(\omega)\) + \(\omega=4\pi Rc/n^3\) \(\Rightarrow R_\infty=me^4/(8\varepsilon_0^2 h^3 c)\).

**SG force.** \(U=-\mu\cdot B\Rightarrow F_z=\mu_z\partial B_z/\partial z\). Drop \(F_x,F_y\).

**SO.** \(\mathbf{B}_L\propto\mathbf{L}/r^3\), Thomas 1/2, \(\mathbf{L}\cdot\mathbf{S}=\frac12(J^2-L^2-S^2)\), \(\langle r^{-3}\rangle\propto Z^3/[n^3\ell(\ell+1/2)(\ell+1)]\).

**Dipole \(\Delta m\).** \(z\Rightarrow\int e^{i(m_a-m_b)\phi}d\phi\Rightarrow\Delta m=0\); \(x\pm iy\Rightarrow\Delta m=\pm 1\). Parity of \(Y_{\ell m}\) is \((-1)^\ell\).
