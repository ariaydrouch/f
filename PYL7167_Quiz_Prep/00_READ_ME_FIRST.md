# PYL7167 / AMP 7167 — Quiz Study Pack

Atomic physics quiz prep built from your lecture notes (AMP 7167, 24 pp.), the 18 practice problems (high emphasis), and the standard books your notes cite: **Haken & Wolf**, **Bransden & Joachain**, **H. E. White**, **C. J. Foot**, plus Jackson (classical radiation) and Kittel (Wannier exciton).

This pack is scoped to **what is actually in the notes + practice set**. That is the atomic-physics first half of the IIT Delhi AMP syllabus (hydrogen / alkali / helium / fine structure / SG / Zeeman–Stark / radiation / linewidths). Molecular spectroscopy (FTIR, Raman, Franck–Condon) is later-course material and is **not** drilled here.

## What will almost certainly be asked

The practice set is a blueprint. Expect a mix of:

- A **numerical** Rydberg / reduced-mass / exotic-atom / Doppler / natural-width problem (1, 2, 3, 5, 8).
- A **diagram** problem: Na 3d→3p Zeeman + Paschen–Back + polarisation (9), or H Stark n=3→2 (12), or Li vs H levels (16), or SG deflection (11).
- A **coupling / term-symbol** problem: LS (10, 13) or jj (14).
- A **formal** bit: Unsold (17), helium spin eigenfunction (15), or T/F (18).

If a derivation is requested, the four that lectures actually set up are: correspondence-principle Rydberg constant, SG force from \(U=-\boldsymbol{\mu}\cdot\mathbf{B}\), spin–orbit \(\boldsymbol{L}\cdot\boldsymbol{S}\) shift, and first-order TDPT → dipole selection rules.
